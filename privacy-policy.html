Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

EV Charger Locator respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Email address
  • Encrypted (hashed) password
  • Approximate device location (with your permission)

How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account securely.
  • Display nearby EV charging stations.
  • Improve application performance.

Location Permission

Location is used only to display nearby charging stations and is not used for advertising.

Data Security

Passwords are securely hashed before storage. We use industry-standard security practices to protect user information.

Third-Party Services

This application uses HERE Maps APIs and MongoDB Atlas to provide map data, charging station information, and secure data storage.

Advertising

EV Charger Locator does not display advertisements and does not sell user data.
