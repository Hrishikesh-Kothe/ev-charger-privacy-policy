Request Account Deletion

If you would like to permanently delete your EV Charger Locator account and associated data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to hrishikothe@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Account.
  3. Include the email address you used to register in the app.

What Will Be Deleted

  • Your account information.
  • Your registered email address.
  • Your authentication data.

What Is Not Stored

  • Your live location is never permanently stored.
  • No payment information is collected.
  • No advertising data is collected.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are normally processed within 7 days.
